Drawer

An off-canvas panel over Material's MatDrawer. The mode decides the accessibility contract, not just the animation: over and push render a backdrop and trap focus, so the drawer is a modal dialog; side is part of the layout and is not modal at all. Switch the mode and watch the announced semantics change with it.
